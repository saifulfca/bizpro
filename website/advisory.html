FAQ

Advisory questions

When is the individual income tax return due?

For most individual taxpayers in Bangladesh the deadline is 30 November each year, unless the government extends it. We send reminders well in advance.

Can you take over books that are behind?

Yes. We start with a catch-up project to bring your records up to date, then move you onto a monthly plan.

Do you sign statutory audit reports?

Statutory audit opinions are issued by ICAB-registered audit firms. Bizpro prepares your accounts for audit and coordinates the audit with a licensed firm, so the process is quick and clean.

Which accounting software do you support?

QuickBooks Online is our standard. We also work with Tally, Xero and spreadsheet-based records.
