About Bizpro

Accountants by training. Problem-solvers by practice.

Bizpro began as an accounting practice and grew with its clients’ needs. After filing their taxes, clients asked for help with company compliance, then with selling property, then with building on their land. Today all four services are under one roof.

Mission

Make professional services simple and trustworthy

We give business owners and families in Bangladesh one accountable partner for their finances, compliance and property, with the discipline of a Chartered Accountancy practice.

Vision: to be the most trusted one-stop professional services firm for SMEs and property owners in Bangladesh.
