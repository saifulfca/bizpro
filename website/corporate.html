FAQ

Compliance questions

How long does company registration take?

With complete documents, RJSC incorporation of a private limited company usually takes 7 to 15 working days. Licenses and registrations follow in parallel.

What does a ‘dedicated expert’ mean?

A named Bizpro professional is assigned to your company. They learn your business, prepare your filings and are your day-to-day contact, with review by a partner.

Can you take over from my current accountant?

Yes. We handle the handover, review open filings and fix past gaps before starting your monthly plan.

Do you help close a company?

Yes. We handle voluntary winding up, final tax and VAT clearance, and RJSC strike-off or dissolution.
