Open-book construction

You own the building. You see every taka.

In a traditional land-share deal the developer controls the costs and keeps most of the flats. With Bizpro you keep ownership and control.

Typical developer deal

  • Developer takes a large share of the finished flats
  • Construction cost is hidden from the landowner
  • Material quality decided by the developer
  • Delays with little reporting

Bizpro development partner

  • You keep 100% of the building
  • Every expense shared with bills and receipts
  • You approve materials and finishes
  • Monthly progress and cost reports, audited by accountants

FAQ

Construction questions

How does cost-plus billing work?

You fund the project in stages. We spend on your behalf, record every bill and receipt in a project ledger you can review, and charge our agreed development fee on top of actual cost.

Who supervises quality?

Our partner architect and site engineers inspect each stage: foundation, structure, masonry, MEP and finishing. You receive the inspection reports.

Can I sell some flats to fund construction?

Yes. Bizpro Realty can market flats during construction to help fund the build.

Do you work outside Dhaka?

Yes, subject to a site assessment. Contact us with the land location.

Have land and a plan?

Book a site visit. We will assess the land, discuss your goals and give you a written budget and timeline.
