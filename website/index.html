Questions

Frequently asked

Can I use only one Bizpro service?

Yes. Each wing works on its own. Many clients start with tax filing or a property sale and add other services later.

How are your fees set?

Accounting and compliance work is billed as a fixed monthly plan or a fixed project fee agreed in writing. Property brokerage is success-based, so you pay only when the deal closes. Construction is billed at actual cost plus an agreed development fee.

Do you work with clients outside Dhaka or abroad?

Yes. Most accounting and compliance work is done digitally. Non-resident Bangladeshis often use us to manage property sales and building projects at home.

How do you keep my financial information confidential?

Every engagement starts with a signed engagement letter that includes confidentiality terms. Access to your records is limited to the team working on your file.
