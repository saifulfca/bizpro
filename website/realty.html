FAQ

Property questions

How much does it cost to list my property?

Nothing upfront. Our brokerage fee is agreed with you in writing before marketing starts and is payable only when the sale completes.

I live abroad. Can you still sell my flat?

Yes. With a valid power of attorney we can manage the full process and keep you updated online.

Do you help buyers too?

Yes. Tell us your budget and preferred areas and we will match you with verified listings and check the documents before you pay.

Do you handle rentals?

Our focus is sales and joint-venture land deals. Contact us for commercial leasing requests.

Looking to buy?

Share your budget and preferred location. We will send matching verified listings.
