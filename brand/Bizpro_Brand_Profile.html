Brand Profile

Logo, colours, typography, voice and applications for Bizpro and its four business wings.

Version 1.0 · November 2026 · www.bizpro.com.bd

1. Brand idea

Ledger to Landmark.

Bizpro started with the ledger: accounts, tax and compliance. Clients then trusted it with their biggest assets: property and buildings. The brand connects the two with one promise: the precision and honesty of a Chartered Accountant, applied to everything we do.

ElementDefinition
PurposeMake professional services simple and trustworthy for business owners and families in Bangladesh.
PromiseOne accountable team, with written fees and partner-reviewed work.
PersonalityTrustworthy · Precise · Modern · Approachable
AudienceSME owners, company directors, individual taxpayers, NRBs, property owners, landowners, buyers
Proof pointsSenior CA with 22+ years; Architect and tax partners; DSCC trade license; open-book construction

2. Logo

The mark combines three rising bars (accounts, growth) with a gold arrow that also reads as a roof line (property, building). The wordmark is set in lowercase, with “pro” in Growth Teal.

Primary logo on light backgrounds
Reversed logo on navy or photography
App icon / favicon / social avatar
Minimum size: 24 px / 8 mm
Clear space
Keep space equal to the height of the “b” around the logo. Do not place text or graphics inside it.

Files
website/assets/img/logo.svg, logo-white.svg, logo-mark.svg

Do

Use the supplied SVG files · use the reversed logo on dark backgrounds · keep the tagline only where the logo is 150 px wide or more

Don’t

Stretch or rotate the logo · change the colours · add shadows or outlines · place the primary logo on busy photos · write “BizPro” or “Biz Pro” in text (use “Bizpro”)

5. Voice & messaging

Honest“Audit support, coordinated with a licensed audit firm.”Claims we cannot back with a licence or record

Key messages

  • Master line: From your ledger to your next landmark.
  • Advisory: Accurate books, timely returns and numbers your bank will accept.
  • Corporate: Your company’s compliance, handled from inception to dissolution.
  • Realty: Sell your property faster, to the right buyer, with less hassle.
  • Build: Build on your land with full cost transparency.
