1. Executive summary

Bizpro is an accounting firm at its core, diversified into the services its clients need next. Clients who trust Bizpro with their tax and accounts also need company registration, ongoing compliance, help selling property, and someone to manage construction on their land. Bizpro offers all four under one brand and one relationship.

Bizpro AdvisoryAccounting, QuickBooks, tax, audit support, valuation, feasibility studies
Bizpro CorporateRJSC registration, licenses, VAT, tax, corporate accounting & secretarial on monthly retainer
Bizpro RealtyBrokerage between property owners and buyers, lead generation through digital ads
Bizpro BuildDevelopment partner: builds on the owner’s land at actual cost plus a fee

The model in one sentence

Bizpro owns the client (brand, lead generation, sales, relationship, quality sign-off and billing) and orchestrates delivery through a flexible network of vetted freelancers, partner firms, contractors and deployed staff, keeping a margin between what the client pays and what delivery costs.

Why it can win:

  • Credibility: led by a senior Chartered Accountant with 22 years of practice, with a senior Architect, an MBA income tax professional and a business graduate as partners.
  • Asset-light: most delivery cost is variable, so the firm can scale with demand without carrying a large payroll.
  • Cross-sell flywheel: each wing feeds the others. A tax client becomes a retainer client, a property seller, or a landowner who builds.
  • Trust gap in the market: landowners distrust developers and SMEs struggle to find reliable accountants. A CA-led, open-book brand addresses both.

Hypothetical targets: net revenue of about BDT 1.26 crore in year 1, rising to about BDT 5.6 crore by year 3, with EBITDA margin growing from roughly 28% to 39% (before partner profit share and tax). See Section 10.

Structure gap to resolve

The license is a sole proprietorship with a consultancy classification, while the business is described as a partnership running brokerage and construction. Before scaling, the partners should (a) sign a partnership deed or incorporate a private limited company, and (b) add the correct trade license categories (or separate licenses) for real estate brokerage and construction / development. Construction carries liability that should sit inside a limited-liability entity. See Section 13.

3. The problem and the opportunity

For SMEs and individuals

  • Books kept late or in spreadsheets; returns filed at the last minute.
  • Several separate vendors for registration, VAT, tax and secretarial work, and none of them owns the outcome.
  • Bank loan applications rejected for weak projections and feasibility reports.
  • Full-time qualified accountants are expensive for small firms.

For property owners

  • Selling a flat or land is slow, informal and full of time-wasting enquiries.
  • Owners living abroad (NRBs) cannot manage a sale or a construction project themselves.
  • Traditional land-share deals give the developer a large share of flats and hide the true cost.
  • Little trust in brokers and developers on price and quality.

Opportunity: a single trusted brand, led by a Chartered Accountant, that applies audit-style discipline (documentation, reconciliation, written fees) to every service, including property and construction.

5.2 Bizpro Corporate: one-stop setup & compliance

ElementDescription
ServicesRJSC registration (private limited, OPC, partnership, society); trade license, e-TIN, BIN, IRC / ERC; monthly VAT returns; withholding and corporate tax; corporate accounting and payroll; company secretarial from inception to dissolution (registers, minutes, AGM, annual returns, share transfers, winding up)

5.3 Bizpro Realty: property brokerage

ElementDescription
RoleIntermediary between owners / sellers of flats, land and commercial property and prospective buyers
Lead generationGoogle Search and Facebook / Instagram ads per listing; property portals; WhatsApp broadcast; network channels (banks, NRB groups, existing clients); direct marketing
Pricing mechanism (internal)The owner states a bottom price (the net amount they accept). Bizpro adds its margin to set the asking price and markets at that price. Bizpro’s earning is the difference between the final sale price and the owner’s bottom price, or an agreed commission, whichever the mandate specifies.
DeliveryIn-house property advisors handle owners and buyers. Photography, ad management and site-visit support can be outsourced. Legal document checks through a panel lawyer.

Make the margin structure defensible

The bottom-price model is workable only if the owner agrees to it in a written mandate that states the owner’s net price and that Bizpro keeps the amount above it as its fee. Without that, the owner may later claim a hidden mark-up, which is a reputational and legal risk for a CA-led brand. Recommended: an exclusive mandate for 60–90 days with either (a) a disclosed net-price clause, or (b) a fixed commission (for example 1.5–3% of sale value, hypothetical) plus a performance bonus above a target price.

5.4 Bizpro Build: development partner

ElementDescription
RoleBuilds residential, commercial or mixed-use buildings on behalf of the landowner, who keeps ownership of the whole building
Revenue modelCost-plus: actual expenses billed as-is with receipts, plus a Bizpro construction / development fee (hypothetical 8–12% of cost, or a fixed fee per sq ft). Design and approval services billed separately or bundled.
Delivery (internal)Bizpro deploys contractors, sub-contractors, site engineers and labour through contracts. Bizpro staff own owner communication, cost tracking, procurement approval and quality inspections. The partner Architect leads design and QC.
Cash disciplineBizpro should never fund construction from its own balance sheet. The owner funds a separate project bank account in stages ahead of each phase; Bizpro reconciles it monthly and shares the project ledger.
Cross-sellBizpro Realty can sell the owner’s surplus flats during construction to fund the build.

6. Operating model: core team + delivery network

Confidential: do not disclose externally

The delivery network described below is an internal operating choice. Website, proposals and client communication present Bizpro as the single responsible service provider. This is not a misrepresentation as long as Bizpro genuinely takes responsibility for the result, reviews all work, and protects client data, which this section requires.

1. AttractGoogle / Facebook ads, website, referrals, network
2. ConvertBizpro consultation, written scope & fee, engagement letter
3. DeliverFreelancers, partner firms, contractors or deployed staff
4. ReviewBizpro professional checks and signs off
5. Deliver & billBizpro sends output, invoices client, collects

Teal stages are always done by Bizpro. Only stage 3 is sourced externally.

6.1 What Bizpro always owns

  • Brand and lead generation (ads, website, content, referral partners)
  • Client relationship: every call, meeting, WhatsApp chat and email comes from a Bizpro identity
  • Scope, price and contract with the client
  • Quality review and sign-off of every deliverable
  • Regulatory touchpoints: submissions, representation before NBR / RJSC / RAJUK, signing
  • Billing and collection; vendors are never paid by the client directly

6.2 Delivery network

SourceUsed forControls
Upwork / Fiverr freelancersBookkeeping, reconciliations, QuickBooks work, financial models, report drafting, design, ad managementPaid test task; NDA; Bizpro-owned software logins with limited roles; anonymised data where possible; payments kept on-platform per platform terms
Local freelancers / part-time professionalsVAT and tax preparation, on-site deployment to retainer clientsWritten service agreement, confidentiality clause, Bizpro email ID, monthly performance review
Partner firmsStatutory audit (ICAB-registered CA firm), legal / title checks (panel lawyer), structural design reviewReferral or sub-contract agreement; the licensed firm signs where the law requires
Contractors & site staffCivil, MEP and finishing work; site engineers; labourWork contracts with BOQ, rates, retention money, insurance, safety plan, defect liability period

6.3 Vendor management

  1. Onboard: portfolio review, paid test task, reference check, NDA.
  2. Tier: Gold (trusted, complex work), Silver (routine), Trial. Keep at least two vendors per critical skill.
  3. Brief: standard job templates and checklists per service.
  4. Review: maker-checker. Nothing goes to a client without Bizpro review.
  5. Rate: score each job on accuracy, timeliness and communication; drop vendors below threshold.

6.4 Data protection for outsourced work

  • Work inside Bizpro-owned accounts (QuickBooks, Google Workspace / Microsoft 365) with role-based access, never on a freelancer’s own account.
  • Share the minimum data needed; mask client names, NID and bank details for offshore tasks.
  • Revoke access at job end; keep an access log.
  • Client engagement letters should allow use of “sub-contractors under Bizpro’s supervision and confidentiality obligations”, which protects Bizpro without naming platforms.

8.3 Cross-sell flywheel

Tax / accounts client → company registration and retainer → valuation or loan feasibility → property sale (Realty) → construction on family land (Build) → flats sold during construction (Realty) → tax on the gain (Advisory). Every client file is tagged with “next likely need” in the CRM.

12. Quality control & client protection

  • Engagement letter for every client: scope, fee, timeline, client responsibilities, confidentiality, sub-contracting clause, limitation of liability.
  • Maker-checker on every deliverable; review checklist stored with the file.
  • Deadline calendar per client (tax, VAT, RJSC, trade license, AGM) with 30 / 7 / 1-day reminders.
  • Realty: written mandate, title / mutation / khatian checks by panel lawyer before marketing, buyer advance held as per agreement.
  • Build: separate project bank account per owner, BOQ-based contracts, three quotes for major purchases, stage inspections signed by the Architect, monthly cost and photo report, 5–10% retention from contractors, insurance and site safety plan, compliance with the Bangladesh National Building Code (BNBC 2020) and approved plans.
  • Complaints: partner-level escalation within 48 hours.
